# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want DevReach to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Credit other people's ideas and contributions.
- Accept that first-time contributors are learning; help them.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, including using DevReach issues or discussions to promote unrelated products.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official community channel) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or by opening a [private security advisory](https://github.com/devanathsugavasi/devreach/security/advisories/new) if no other channel is available. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is harmful.
