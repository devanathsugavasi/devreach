# Example Launch Campaign

> How several DevReach skills chain together for one launch, using the fictional project in [`sample-project.md`](sample-project.md). Every step produces drafts; the developer reviews and publishes each one themselves.

| Step | When | Skill | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 weeks before | `audience-research` | Primary segment: backend teams that send logs to third-party services. Positioning statement. 6 interview questions. |
| 2 | 3 weeks before | `open-source-growth` | README top rewritten; CONTRIBUTING with real commands; 3 good first issues ("add Twilio token pattern", etc.). |
| 3 | 2 weeks before | `content-planner` | 2 posts/week for 4 weeks, anchored to launch day. |
| 4 | 1 week before | `technical-storyteller` | Article: "Why my secret-redaction regex flagged every UUID (and how entropy fixed it)". |
| 5 | Launch week | `launch-announcement` | v1.0.0 release notes, announcement, 60-second demo script, launch checklist. |
| 6 | Launch week | `community-launch` | Shortlist (r/Python, Show HN, a Python Discord) with distinct drafts and a sequence. |
| 7 | Launch week | `multi-platform-content` | The article adapted for LinkedIn and X. |
| 8 | Launch +1 day | `community-engagement` | Replies to HN comments, including a calm reply to "just use a log scrubber in your log pipeline". |
| 9 | Launch +2 weeks | `outreach-writer` | One message to a logging-library maintainer about linking quietlog. |
| 10 | Launch +2 weeks | `growth-review` | Referrers vs. installs; experiment: add a "Works with structlog" section. |

## What this campaign deliberately avoids

- Posting the same text in many places on the same day.
- Asking anyone to upvote or star.
- Messaging people who haven't shown interest in the problem.
- Any claim not in the project's fact sheet.
